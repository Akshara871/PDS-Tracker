About PDS Tracker

The Public Distribution System (PDS) plays an important role in providing essential food commodities to eligible beneficiaries.

PDS Tracker aims to improve transparency by providing information about ration shops, commodity stock, shop status and beneficiary complaints through a centralized digital platform.

The system helps beneficiaries stay informed and supports dealers and administrators in managing distribution activities more effectively.
